# LAND under a frozen position, with PX4's flight modes (issue 141)

Status: results note, 2026-10-31. Written by Claude from the recordings; the maintainer decides
what it means for ADR 0003 and ADR 0004. No ADR text or recommender change is proposed here.

## What was run

The LAND cells of the issue 81 matrix, repeated with PX4's flight-mode events recorded: a frozen
GNSS position that stays latched, one vehicle, a 600 m survey route, cruise speeds of 5 and 12 m/s,
LAND commanded 5 or 30 s after the fault, three runs per cell, each in a freshly restarted
simulator (`sim/record_land_diagnosis.sh`, PX4 pinned at a427a2e). Recordings with ground truth
are in `tests/replay/data/experiments/land-diagnosis/`. Two runs were thrown away and recorded
again: one had no ground truth, and one was ended by a slow simulator before the vehicle reached
the zone (`sim.recording_check` now rejects both).

Speeds are horizontal, from the true position, over samples within 1 s either side of the moment
(a proposal). *Speed below 3 m* is as in `docs/validation/frozen-position-matrix.md`; "none" means
no usable window of samples between 3 m and the ground. The tables are generated by
`python -m eval.land_diagnosis tests/replay/data/experiments/land-diagnosis`, and a test fails if
they differ from the recordings. Counts and ranges only, never rates.

## What the recordings show

- **PX4 enters LAND at once.** In all 12 runs the vehicle was in MISSION at the command and
  entered LAND 0.0 to 1.0 s later. The fast touchdown is not a delay in the mode change.
- **The speed at the command is set by the delay.** With a 5 s delay it is 6.0 (5 m/s cruise) and
  11.8 to 12.2 m/s (12 m/s cruise), about the cruise speed. With a 30 s delay it is 21.1 to 28.1
  and 14.0 to 18.7 m/s, well above cruise: the vehicle keeps speeding up while its position is
  frozen, as ADR 0003 found.
- **LAND mode did not slow the vehicle in its first second.** The speed at LAND mode is within
  1.5 m/s of the speed at the command in all 12 runs.
- **Touchdown speed follows the speed at the command only in part.** At 5 m/s and a 5 s delay the
  speed goes from 4.6 to 5.1 m/s at LAND mode to 3.7 to 4.0 m/s below 3 m, the nearest to a
  controlled landing. At 12 m/s and a 5 s delay it ranges 4.4 to 13.7 m/s from 11.6 to 12.2 m/s,
  and in one run it is higher below 3 m than at LAND mode. At a 30 s delay it ranges 2.4 to
  21.7 m/s from 14.0 to 28.1 m/s, and 2 of the 3 runs at 5 m/s have no window at all.
- **The vehicle reached the ground (altitude 0.5 m or less) 5.4 to 12.5 s after the command in all
  12 runs** (ground truth).
- **Three runs show `UNKNOWN` flight modes between LAND entries**, all after touchdown
  (`stuck-land-v12-d30-1`, `stuck-land-v12-d5-1`, `stuck-land-v5-d30-1`). The last also ends with a
  ground-truth altitude of about -11 km, so its late samples are not usable. They do not affect
  the touchdown figures; why they appear is not known.

## What they do not show

- Why the speed stays high or comes back after LAND begins. Read from the ground-truth traces
  (not in the tables): in the six runs with a 5 s delay the speed 3 s after the command is 0.7 to
  4.4 m/s and 6 and 9 s after it 4.1 to 15.0 m/s again. Momentum alone would not give that, but the
  recordings carry no estimator or setpoint data, so what the controller was doing is not known.
- Whether the frozen position, momentum or the simulated vehicle is the larger cause.
- Anything about a real vehicle.

## For the maintainer

The result leaves the amended ADR 0003 and ADR 0004 wording as it is (LAND is quickest to the
ground, not shown to be a controlled landing). The delay is the one variable here that changed
the speed at the command, so acting early is the only lever these runs show. Finding the cause
needs PX4's own logs (ulog) kept during a run, which would be a new issue.

## Limits

Three runs per cell, one simulator, one vehicle, PX4's `stuck` fault model, a 220 s cap, and a
speed window and a 5 m/s controlled threshold that are proposals.
