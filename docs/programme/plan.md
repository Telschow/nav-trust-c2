## The re-plan, honestly

Nothing slipped. The engineering milestones were finished far ahead of their dates, in about three
days of repository history (the maintainer planned over about three weeks beforehand). The
remaining schedule risk is people and writing time, not engineering: the usability sessions, the
product documents, the video and the write-up.

Scope changes made on the way, with reasons:
- **No ROS 2.** The spec allows falling back to MAVSDK only. Recordings are JSON lines.
- **PX4 pinned to an unreleased `main` commit**, because GPS failure injection for Gazebo does not
  exist in v1.17.0 or v1.18.0-rc1.
- **LAND for a frozen position**, after ground truth showed the vehicle flies away (ADR 0003).
- **A calibrated position bound**, after NT-03 turned out not to be met (PR 37).
- **Added:** a held-out validation, ground truth, a requirements trace with a test.
- **Not done:** `link_loss` scenario, a noise or offset injector for gradual drift, reuse of the
  approval pattern from personal-ai, mission-completion measurement.
